Step 1 — Your Snowflake trial account

Needed before 9:45 · about 5 minutes

Snowflake trial signup — use this link, not a signup page you find yourself.

Leave the region exactly as it is — AWS Asia Pacific (Tokyo). It looks wrong from Perth. It isn’t. Changing it will break the lab.

Choose Enterprise edition. Check the activation email, sign in to Snowsight, and confirm your role says ACCOUNTADMIN in the top right.

Already created a trial? Keep using it. Ignore this step.

Step 2 — The CoCo CLI

Needed before 9:45 · about 5 minutes

macOS, Linux or WSL — paste into a terminal:

curl -LsS https://ai.snowflake.com/static/cc-scripts/install.sh | sh

Windows — paste into PowerShell:

irm https://ai.snowflake.com/static/cc-scripts/install.ps1 | iex

Then run cortex and follow the prompts to connect to your trial account. Do this one before Step 3 and Desktop will pick up the connection on its own.

Step 3 — CoCo Desktop

Fine to leave until the 9:30 break

CoCo Desktop downloads — macOS or Windows. On Windows pick the User installer; it needs no administrator rights, so a locked-down laptop is not a blocker.

Launch it and run the four-step setup. If you did Step 2 first, your connection is detected automatically — just select it.

Then click your connection name in the top bar and Set Default Warehouse. Don’t skip that one — it’s the single most common reason queries fail later in the morning.

Step 4 — Badge enrolment

Not needed today · do it whenever

You’ll need a Snowflake Partner Network login. No account? Enrol at spn.snowflake.com first.

Then sign in and enrol in the Snowflake Partner CoCo Foundation Learning Track. If that link misbehaves, go to learn.snowflake.com/en/partners and find the track under Featured Learning.

Once you’re enrolled, stop. No pre-reading. We cover Part 1 today and Part 2 is the lab we run together. We’ll walk through claiming the badge at the close.

The 30-second test

Open the CLI with cortex, and if you’ve installed it, CoCo Desktop too. In each one, type:

What Snowflake account, user and role am I connected as?

If you get an answer naming your trial account and ACCOUNTADMIN, you’re ready.

If you see either of these, stop and flag it — don’t try to fix it:

Cortex Code is not enabled or the usage limit has been reached
Access denied for trial accounts

These mean AI features need switching on for your account. It’s a known thing, it is not something you’ve done wrong, and it takes us a minute to sort. Just show one of us the screen.
