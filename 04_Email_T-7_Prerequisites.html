PREREQUISITE 2 — 10 MINUTES
Install the CoCo CLI

macOS and Linux (including WSL) — in a terminal:

curl -LsS https://ai.snowflake.com/static/cc-scripts/install.sh | sh

Windows — in PowerShell:

irm https://ai.snowflake.com/static/cc-scripts/install.ps1 | iex

Then launch it and connect to the trial account you just created:

cortex

CoCo will walk you through connecting on first launch. Once you're at the chat prompt, type this to confirm everything is wired up:

What Snowflake account, user and role am I connected as?

If it answers with your trial account and ACCOUNTADMIN, you're done.

Reference: CoCo CLI documentation

PREREQUISITE 3 — 10 MINUTES
Install CoCo Desktop

1. Download it for macOS or Windows:

snowflake.com — CoCo Desktop downloads

On Windows you'll be offered two installers — choose the User installer. It installs for you only and doesn't need administrator rights, which matters if your laptop is managed by your IT team.

2. Launch it and complete the four-step setup: Welcome → Connect → View → Theme.

Because you did Prerequisite 2 first, Desktop will detect your existing CoCo CLI connection automatically at the Connect step. Just select it and click Next — no re-entering credentials.

If no connection appears, click Add connection and fill in:

  • Account identifier — in the form orgname-accountname
  • Username — your Snowflake login name
  • Authentication method — choose Local OAuth, which signs you in through your browser

To find your account identifier: sign in at app.snowflake.com, click your avatar in the bottom-left, and select Connect a tool to Snowflake.

3. Pick a view and theme. Choose Agent view and either theme — both are changeable later. Click Get Started.

4. Set a default warehouse. Click your connection name in the top bar, choose Set Default Warehouse, and pick one. Skipping this is the most common cause of queries failing on the day.

Then check it works: type this into the chat box and confirm you get a sensible answer.

What Snowflake account, user and role am I connected as?

Reference: CoCo Desktop installation and authentication
