Welcome

Everything you need for the morning lives on this page. You’ll go from zero to a working Cortex Agent in a few hours — generating data, standing up Cortex Search and a semantic view, and deploying an agent you can actually demo.

It’s built for customer-facing partner roles. Whether you sell, architect or deliver, you should leave able to tell the Agentic Control Plane story credibly in a client conversation — and with your own custom demo to show.

Arrived and not set up yet?

No problem at all, and you won’t be the only one. Start here — about 15 minutes and grab any of the Snowflake team on the floor.
