Risk register

#RiskSevMitigationOwner
R1 Attendees arrive without a working trial account or CoCo installed. Historically the single biggest killer of hands-on sessions. 25 self-provisioned trials means 25 chances to fail. HIGH Prerequisites in both pre-event emails with an explicit verification step. Door question at sign-in. Dedicated triage desk 08:00–08:30. Working break at 09:30 with all SEs on the floor. Backup account available to pair onto. Brian Orloff
R2 CoWork is unavailable or inconsistent on trial accounts. Module 7 and the hackathon bonus both depend on it. HIGH Verify on a fresh trial at dry-run (27 Oct). If unreliable: Module 7 becomes a front-of-room demo on the backup account, and the hackathon bonus is scored on an SE-assisted demo. Both documents already warn participants not to debug this in-session. Luke
R3 Data generation in Module 1 overruns. Generating long transcripts is the slowest step and blocks everything after it. HIGH Already reduced 150→60 transcripts. Guide tells participants 40+ is enough to proceed and to top up rather than restart. Lab lead moves the room on at 10:02 regardless. Backup account lets anyone catastrophically behind re-join at Module 2. Luke
R4 Venue network blocks or throttles CoCo. Corporate-grade filtering at a conference venue is common and would end the session. HIGH Live-fire test on venue wifi at 07:40, before anyone arrives. Confirm with Cliftons in advance that there is no outbound filtering and ask for the guest bandwidth figure. Have mobile hotspots as fallback for at least the six team accounts. Luke
R5 Five non-technical attendees stall in a CLI-first lab and disengage, or become passengers in the hackathon. MED Driver/navigator pairing built into the lab guide with an explicit note to sales and leadership attendees. Teams are allocated with exactly one non-technical member each. Hackathon defines a business-owner role that genuinely needs them. Brian Orloff
R6 Published quickstart changes between now and the event, or product behaviour drifts. Snowflake states the guides are not maintained on an ongoing basis. MED Full dry-run on 27 Oct against a fresh account. Lab guide links to the live quickstart as authoritative. Review the guide after dry-run and correct any divergence. Luke
R7 Pitches overrun and the close is lost. Six teams in 24 minutes has almost no slack, and the badge message lives in the last 3 minutes. MED Visible countdown, hard cut at 3:00, order drawn at random. If running late, judges confer during the final pitch rather than afterwards. The badge-claim message is never cut — drop judges' questions instead. Brian Orloff
R8 Badge link does not work for external partner logins, so nobody can claim what we committed to. MED Test the enablement URL from outside the Snowflake SSO boundary before the T-7 email. If partners cannot self-enrol, obtain the correct enrolment route from the partner training team and put that in the wrap-up email instead. Brian Orloff
R9 No-shows unbalance the teams. 25 registered, expect 18–22 on the day. LOW Collapse to five or even four teams at 10:45 rather than running undersized ones. Fewer teams also buys back pitch time. Brian Orloff
R10 A participant loads confidential client data into a trial account during the hackathon. MED Explicit non-negotiable rule in the hackathon brief. Restate verbally at team formation. Floor leads intervene immediately if they see it. Brian Orloff

Judging logistics

Three judges score independently, then confer for three minutes. Published rubric: Business problem clarity 30 · Agent quality 30 · Pitch 25 · CoWork bonus up to 15. Tie-breaker: whichever solution the judges would most readily take into a live client conversation.

Every team gets one sentence of specific praise at the close. Twenty-five people from ten partner firms will each report back internally on how this event went. Five teams leaving feeling like losers is a worse commercial outcome than any single prize is worth. The praise column is not decoration — fill it in during the pitches.

Judges' questions worth asking

  • What did CoCo do here, and what did you hand-write?
  • Show me your AGENTS.md — what did it stop you doing?
  • Ask it something your data cannot answer. What happens?
  • What would it take to run this on a real client's data?
  • What broke, and what did you do about it?
