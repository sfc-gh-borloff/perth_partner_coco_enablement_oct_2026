Corporate laptop lockdown — Deloitte, EY and other large firms may block CLI installs or terminal access entirely. MED Called out in the first email so it surfaces over a week early, not at 08:05. The Windows User installer for Desktop needs no administrator rights and absorbs some of these; the CLI has no equivalent escape hatch, so a fully locked-down laptop will be Desktop-only. The lab works that way — only the Module 6 recommendation changes. Paired working means one locked-down laptop per pair is survivable. Have two or three loaner laptops if you can source them.
Five non-technical attendees disengage during a CLI lab. MED Paired driver/navigator model with explicit guidance in the lab guide. Seat each with a strong Delivery person. Brief SEs by name. The hackathon business-owner role is designed for them, and carries 30% of the marks.
Pitch slot overruns. Six teams in 25 minutes is tight and has no slack. MED Three minutes is already the shortened format. Visible countdown timer. MC cuts at 3:00 without apology. Brief judges to ask one question only. If you end up with fewer teams, give the time back as questions.
Semantic view or agent creation fails on some accounts due to role or privilege differences. MED Module 0 verifies privileges explicitly before anyone starts. Floor 1 owns a written triage runbook for the common failures. The guide tells people to stop and raise a hand rather than debug alone.
Technically strong attendees finish early and disengage. LOW Stretch tasks are built into Modules 3, 5 and 6. Steer early finishers to help their neighbours — it is the fastest way to lift the room, and tell them so.

Contingency calls — decide these in advance

Room is 15 min behind at 10:34

Cut Module 7. Demo CoWork from the front in three minutes. Keep Module 6 — the hackathon depends on AGENTS.md. Protect the 10:45 break.

A third of the room has no working agent at 10:45

Extend the break by 10 minutes, shorten the hackathon to 50 min, and pair teams onto fewer accounts. A hackathon with 4 working accounts beats one with 6 broken ones.

CoWork is unavailable on the day

Front-of-room demo on a known-good account. Announce the bonus is withdrawn before the hackathon starts, not during judging.

Network fails entirely

Nothing recovers a hands-on lab from this. Pivot to Matthew demoing on a hotspot, convert the hackathon to a whiteboard design exercise on the same three tracks, and send the lab home as homework with the badge link.

A team's live demo fails mid-pitch

MC moves them to the end of the order; Floor 2 works the problem. They get their three minutes after the last team.

Fewer than 16 attendees show

Four teams, restore pitches to 5 minutes, and use the recovered time for open Q&A on client positioning — which is what senior attendees want most anyway.
